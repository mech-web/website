Build with Mech

Mech is a programming language for building data-driven, reactive systems such as robots, games, simulations, and user interfaces. It treats data and the relationships between data as the center of a program.

D4

Language concepts

Mech is declarative, statically typed, embeddable, and designed around matrices, functions, patterns, and state machines. Its purpose is to make live programs easier to understand, test, debug, visualize, and connect to the environments where they run.
